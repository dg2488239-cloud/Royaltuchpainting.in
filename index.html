FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get a painting quotation?

Use the form above or WhatsApp 9035376925. A site visit helps us understand the exact work and provide a more accurate quotation.

Do you provide repainting?

Yes. Repainting can include cleaning, sanding, surface repair where required, primer and paint coats depending on the selected system.

How is painting cost calculated?

Pricing depends on area, surface condition, preparation work, paint product, number of coats and other requirements. The calculator is only an approximate starting point.

Do you take apartment and commercial projects?

Yes. Royal Tuch Painting can handle homes, apartments, villas, offices and commercial painting projects.
